Smart Queue New

Start as many papers and tasks as you like. Whatever cannot start yet - because QBG is busy with something else - waits here and starts by itself, one at a time, in the order you added it.

What is new
  • Many papers in one go: on Autopilot or New batch choose all the files and tick "Each file is a separate paper". Each becomes its own batch and they run one after another.
  • No more "Another QBG portal job is already running": question creation, translation, solutions, tagging, video tagging, Verify, the bots and every Upload or Retry button join this line instead of being refused.
  • Start another at any time: every task page has a "Start another" button; what you start while something runs waits here.

How the Smart Queue works

  • Two lines run side by side: reading papers, and work on QBG (uploads, translation, tagging, solutions, videos, Verify, Autopilot). Each line runs one at a time; when one finishes, the next in that line starts by itself. A task started while its line is free starts at once, as before.
  • A paper's own Upload waits until that paper has been read.
  • Pressing Upload, Retry or Start twice does not add it twice: it keeps its place.
  • What you asked for is checked again when its turn comes; if the paper or run changed meanwhile, the card says why it did not start.
  • A paper that fails is reported and the queue moves on to the next.
  • Pressing Stop on the paper that is running puts the queue on hold, so the next one does not start over your Stop. So does an expired sign-in, and closing Studio. "Continue the queue" lets it go on.
  • A waiting paper can be removed; nothing has been created for it yet.
  • In Autopilot each paper goes all the way to QBG. In New batch each paper is read and waits at its Preview for your Upload.
