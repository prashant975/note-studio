Why there is no button here that sends anything again

Nothing on this screen sends a question again. Every line above is a record of something that has already happened on the live portal.

Nothing will be sent again automatically either, and there is no button on this screen that will do it. That is on purpose: sending the same question a second time puts a duplicate in front of students, and that cannot be taken back from here.
