How a batch works, and what the states mean

A batch is one faculty paper. You give QBG One the question document and the answer key, it reads them into a list of questions, you check every question one at a time, and only the ones you approve are created on the QBG portal. Nothing reaches the portal until you ask for it.

Reading the paper means the two Word files are still being turned into a question list. Ready to review means that list is waiting for you. Being reviewed means you have started going through it. Created means at least one question from it is live on the portal. Needs attention means reading the paper stopped part way and the report says why.

Progress counts questions that are live on the portal against every question read out of the paper - so a paper you have only half reviewed sits at a low bar even though nothing is wrong.

If a paper says its review sheet is missing, the folder was moved or deleted on this computer and there is nothing left to open. Start a new batch from the same two Word files to read the paper again - or, if a colleague has already read it elsewhere, choose New batch and bring in their finished folder.
