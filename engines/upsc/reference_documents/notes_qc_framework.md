# Notes QC Framework (PW OnlyIAS)

> Human-readable policy source for the QC engine. The production system loads
> the machine-readable form in `config/upsc_qc_rules.yaml` and
> `config/upsc_qc_sources.yaml` — this document is reference only and is never
> parsed at run time. Transcribed from "DCN AI REPORT" (Daily Class Notes);
> place the original DOCX/PDF alongside this file when distributing.

Errors in the notes are classified into three categories:

1. **Factual Errors** – Incorrect facts, data, dates, names, figures, or
   information not consistent with authentic sources.
2. **Conceptual and Flow Errors** – Incorrect explanation, interpretation,
   logic, or understanding of a concept, process, or principle.
3. **English and Grammatical Errors** – Grammar, spelling, punctuation,
   sentence structure, word usage, or clarity of language.

## Factual Error

1. **Article/Schedule/Amendment/judgment verification:** checked against
   standard texts (Laxmikanth, D.D. Basu, Bare Acts); misquotes or imprecise
   legal phrasing flagged. 100% of legal/constitutional references match the
   cited standard source verbatim; zero unverified citations remain.
2. **Authentic-source verification:** only authentic sites referenced;
   Wikipedia or coaching-institute material never treated as standard sources.
   Only official (.gov), standard textbook, or institutional sources are used.
3. **Statistical/data cross-referencing:** economic statistics, demographic
   data, and rankings checked against institutional sources (Economic Survey,
   Union Budget, NITI Aayog, IMF, World Bank, Census); outdated figures
   flagged.
4. **Unsourced-data flagging:** data with no authentic source is flagged
   rather than left unverified.
5. **Subject-specific standard-source matching:** facts checked against the
   exact standard book prescribed for that subject (R.S. Sharma for Ancient
   History, Satish Chandra for Medieval, Bipan Chandra/Spectrum for Modern,
   G.C. Leong for Geography, Ramesh Singh/Vivek Singh for Economy, Nitin
   Singhania for Art & Culture, …).
6. **Consistency of proper nouns/names:** same person, place, dynasty, or
   text spelled identically throughout the note.

## Concept/Flow Error

1. **Heading–content mismatch:** all content under a heading falls strictly
   within its stated scope — neither broader nor narrower.
2. **Internal contradiction between sections:** every core fact or definition
   stated consistently wherever it recurs, including within tables.
3. **Incomplete explanation:** every definition or equivalence has a
   plain-language explanation a student could restate independently.
4. **Formatting check:** every table, list, and formatting element displays
   exactly as intended; no raw syntax or broken formatting visible.

## English/Grammar Error

1. **Spelling error:** title and running headers checked first (most visible);
   the same name spelled differently across the document highlighted.
2. **Grammatical accuracy:** zero grammatical errors affecting meaning or
   clarity remain.
3. **Meaningful sentences:** every sentence independently understandable.
   Simple, clear, easy-to-understand language; no complex vocabulary or
   lengthy sentence structures.
