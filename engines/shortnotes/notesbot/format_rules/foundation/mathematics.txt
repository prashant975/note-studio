FOUNDATION MATHS NOTES (class 9-10) - theory plus the teacher's solved examples.
- Theory first in each section: definitions, theorems, formulas and identities
  with their conditions, as short points.
- Then that section's solved examples in worked_examples, in the order the
  teacher solves them: the full question with its numbers, every step of the
  teacher's working (one step per entry, each equation in $...$), and the final
  answer. Do not skip or merge steps the teacher shows.
- Keep each example under the concept it practises. Do not turn an example's
  working into theory bullets as well.
