Fonts of the typeset notes (note_studio/typeset), all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
They are the faces of the B.Sc. Wallah sample notes (LaTeX, pdfTeX), read from those PDFs:

  Lato-Regular/Bold/Italic/BoldItalic.ttf   Lato 2.015, text (the LaTeX "lato" package)   google/fonts ofl/lato
  cmunss/cmunsx/cmunsi/cmunso.otf             CMU Sans Serif (Bold Extended, Oblique), box   CTAN fonts/cm-unicode
                                              titles and diagram labels (LaTeX \sffamily: SFSX/SFSS)
  Hind-Regular/Bold.ttf                       Hind 2.x, Devanagari in Hindi notes            google/fonts ofl/hind

Formulas use matplotlib's own Computer Modern (cmr10, cmmi10, cmsy10, cmex10), as LaTeX does.
