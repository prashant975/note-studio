Main ek merged PDF provide kar raha hu jisme ek pure chapter ke multiple lectures ke notes hain. Tumhe ek scriptwriter ki tarah act karna hai aur do doston (jaise Raju aur Ram) ke beech ek engaging, podcast-style conversation likhni hai. In dono doston ne abhi-abhi Rahul Sir se ye pura chapter padh kar khatam kiya hai.
Context: Ye dono pure chapter ka ek quick wrap-up aur revision kar rahe hain. Wo discuss kar rahe hain ki chapter ki shuruwat kaise hui thi, alag-alag lectures me concepts kaise build up hue, aur PDF ke core themes kya hain.
Guidelines for the Script:
Language: Pura conversation ekdum natural Hinglish (Hindi written in English alphabet) me hona chahiye.
Scientific Terminology: Strictly dhyaan rakhna ki saari scientific terms, definitions, aur technical words pure English me hi rahe. Unko kisi bhi haal me Hindi me translate mat karna.
Vibe & Vocabulary: Conversation ka tone highly energetic, relatable aur doston wali thodi mazaak-masti se bhara hona chahiye. Students wale slangs use karo jaise: "bhai ye funda chamak gaya", "ye concept to khatarnaak tha", "dimag ki batti jal gayi", "bouncers jaa rahe the but Sir ne bacha liya", "pura chapter link ho gaya", "kya smooth landing thi", etc.
Appreciation for Rahul Sir : Beech-beech me Rahul Sir ke teaching style ko appreciate karna hai. Highlight karo ki Sir ne mushkil topics ko kitne aasan tarike se break down kiya, Lecture 1 se aakhiri lecture ko kaise connect kiya, aur pure chapter ka dar khatam kar diya. Aise dialogues use karo jaise: "Rahul Sir ne kya mast tarike se bataya tha..." ya "Sir ki wajah se hi ye chapter halwa laga."
Flow: Conversation ki starting casual banter se honi chahiye (ki finally ye lamba chapter khatam hua). Fir PDF me diye gaye main scientific concepts par detail me jao (tough aur interesting parts discuss karo). End me dono ke andar ek confidence aana chahiye aur wrap-up aisi vibe ke sath karo ki ab test me aag laga denge/phod denge.
Mere dwara diye gaye PDF ko ache se analyze karo, usme se main concepts extract karo aur in rules ko follow karte hue ek mast Hinglish script generate karo.
